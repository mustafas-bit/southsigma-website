Exits and stake sales

For owners who want to sell, in full or in part.

Selling a business you have built is a decision most owners make once. We advise owners and shareholders through the whole process, and we identify, screen and introduce acquirers who fit the business, its people and the terms you want.

Full exit

All shareholders sell and ownership passes to the acquirer. This suits owners who are retiring, who have no successor in the business, or who want to realise the value they have built.

Partial exit

One or more partners sell their stake while the others stay. The buyer may be a remaining partner, a new investor or a strategic acquirer. This suits partners whose plans have diverged, and businesses that want a new shareholder alongside the founders.

What South Sigma does

  • Establishes what each owner wants from the sale: price, timing, any continuing role, and the future of the staff and the name.
  • Prepares the business for sale: the financial record, a valuation, and a clear account of what an acquirer is buying.
  • Identifies and screens acquirers, including strategic buyers, financial investors and, for a stake sale, the remaining partners.
  • Approaches the shortlisted acquirers in confidence and manages the exchange of information.
  • Compares offers on price, terms and certainty, and supports the negotiation through to completion alongside your lawyers.
  • For a partial exit, helps the partners agree the basis of valuation and the terms between those leaving and those staying.

We run the process in confidence. The business is not named to any acquirer until you have approved the approach.

Market entry and partnerships

Three routes into the Gulf and wider Middle East.

Partner

You enter through a strategic partnership with a local business, such as a distributor, an agent, a licensee or a joint venture partner.

When it fits

  • You want to test demand before committing significant capital.
  • A local business already has the customers, channels or operating capability you need.
  • You can accept shared control in return for a faster start.

What South Sigma does

  • Sets the partner criteria with you, builds a longlist and screens it down to a shortlist.
  • Checks each shortlisted partner in person: ownership, reputation and capacity to perform.
  • Prepares the commercial terms: territory, exclusivity, targets, governance and exit.
  • Writes the joint venture business plan and the first-year operating plan.

Acquire

You enter by buying all or part of a local business that is already trading.

When it fits

  • You need customers, staff, premises and an established operation from the first day.
  • Speed matters more to you than building in your own way.
  • You have the capital and management capacity to integrate a business in a new market.

What South Sigma does

  • Defines the acquisition criteria, builds a target longlist and screens it.
  • Carries out due diligence on the market position, customers, contracts and financial record.
  • Values the business and models the price range.
  • Writes the integration plan for the first 100 days.

Build

You set up your own presence, with your own entity, people and premises.

When it fits

  • You want full control of the brand, the pricing and the customer relationship.
  • No suitable partner or target exists, or the ones that do would cost too much.
  • You can fund the period before the new operation covers its own costs.

What South Sigma does

  • Produces a feasibility study covering demand, competitors, pricing, costs and investment.
  • Compares locations and set-up structures on commercial grounds.
  • Builds the financial model: set-up cost, running cost, break-even and cash requirement.
  • Writes the launch plan: organisation, hiring sequence, suppliers, premises and timetable.

If you have not chosen a route

A market-entry options assessment compares Partner, Acquire and Build for your business in the markets you are considering. It sets out the cost, time, control and risk of each route, tests them against your objectives, and ends with one recommendation and the reasons for it. If the evidence shows that none of the routes makes commercial sense yet, the assessment says so.

Working principles

One senior lead from start to finish

The person who scopes your project with you also leads the work and presents the findings. You have one named senior contact from the first conversation to handover.

Independent advice

We recommend what the evidence supports. If it shows that you should wait, change course or stop, the report says so in plain terms.

Assumptions you can test

Every valuation, model and study sets out its method, its inputs and where they came from. Your team, your auditors and the other side can check the working.

About South Sigma

Privacy policy

    

      
How South Sigma handles personal information.

      

        
This policy is issued by South Sigma Consulting – FZCO ("South Sigma", "we"). It explains what personal information we handle when you visit this website, contact us or interact with our LinkedIn page, why we handle it, and the choices you have. Last updated October 2026.

        
Information you send us

        
This website does not submit forms. The enquiry draft on the contact section stays in your browser until you copy it. When you email us, we receive your name, your email address, your company and role, and whatever else you choose to include. We use this information to reply to you, to assess whether we can help, and to follow up on your enquiry.

        
Information collected when you visit

        
Like most websites, the server that hosts this site may record technical details of each visit, such as the pages requested, the date and time, the browser and operating system, the referring page and the IP address. This information is used to keep the website secure and working, and to understand in aggregate how it is used. We do not use it to identify individual visitors.

        
Our LinkedIn page

        
We have a company page on LinkedIn. When you visit, follow or interact with it, LinkedIn collects and uses your personal information under its own privacy policy, and LinkedIn is primarily responsible for that processing, including any cookies and tracking technologies it uses.

        
We see what you choose to make visible to us there: your profile name and public profile details, your comments and reactions on our posts, and any messages you send to our page. We use this to reply to you and to communicate with people who follow us. If a conversation that starts on LinkedIn becomes an enquiry, we handle it in the same way as an email enquiry.

        
LinkedIn gives page owners aggregated statistics about visitors and followers, such as numbers of views and broad categories of industry or job function. We cannot identify individuals from these statistics.

        
To exercise your rights over information held by LinkedIn, the most direct route is LinkedIn's own privacy settings and contact channels. You can also contact us, and we will help where we can, including by removing content from our page.

        
Client work

        
If you become a client, we handle the personal information needed to carry out the engagement, under the terms agreed with you in writing. Those terms, and any non-disclosure agreement between us, apply alongside this policy.

        
Who we share information with

        
We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who host our website and email and who act on our instructions, with licensed advisers working on your project where you have agreed to this, and with authorities where the law requires it.

        
How long we keep it

        
We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, or for as long as the law requires us to keep records. If you ask us to delete an enquiry, we will delete it unless we are required to keep it.

        
Security

        
This website is served over an encrypted connection. We apply technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please do not send highly sensitive information by email before we have agreed how to exchange it.
